# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: React with Vinext, selected for a responsive, interactive learning surface and straightforward private deployment.

## Users

The primary user is a beginner learning Mandarin Chinese who needs a guided, low-friction way to connect simplified characters with meaning, pronunciation, pinyin, and handwriting.

## Product Purpose

The app helps a beginner learn simplified Chinese characters by bringing recognition, pronunciation, pinyin, dictionary lookup, handwriting practice, and relevant TrainChinese writing videos into one focused lesson.

Success means the learner can immediately start studying a character, hear it, trace or write it, check its meaning, and watch the writing demonstration without leaving the app.

## Positioning

Each character is treated as one complete learning object: sound, pinyin, meaning, handwriting practice, and an embedded TrainChinese demonstration stay together rather than being split across tools.

## Operating Context

The product is used in short self-study sessions on desktop or mobile. Learning progress and saved-character state are kept locally on the current device.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Prioritize simplified Chinese characters.
- Provide beginner-friendly pinyin, English definitions, short usage examples, and pronunciation playback.
- Include an interactive writing canvas with a faint reference character, undo, and clear controls.
- Play TrainChinese YouTube videos inside the app using YouTube embeds.
- Provide a searchable built-in dictionary surface.
- Represent the characters covered by the TrainChinese channel; exact video-to-character coverage depends on the channel's published video metadata and embeddability.
- No user account or cross-device synchronization is required for this version.

## Evidence on Hand

- TrainChinese YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/@trainchinese/videos
- The user has not supplied proprietary character data, a licensed dictionary dataset, or brand assets.
- The app must not claim exhaustive video coverage unless the channel inventory is successfully verified.

## Product Principles

- Begin with doing: the current character and practice surface appear immediately.
- Keep sound, meaning, form, and motion connected in the same lesson.
- Make every control understandable to a first-time Chinese learner.
- Keep the learner in context; external learning media plays inside the app.
- Preserve momentum through local progress rather than requiring sign-in.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear focus states, keyboard-operable controls, generous touch targets, strong contrast, reduced-motion support, and text labels that do not rely on color alone.
